Last updated: September 22, 2026

SmartCalc Suite respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use our financial and utility calculators, informational articles, and related pages. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

All calculator logic runs client-side in your web browser. Values entered into the SIP, EMI, GST, age, or date tools are processed on your device and are not sent to a SmartCalc Suite server. SmartCalc Suite does not store personal financial data, investment amounts, loan details, tax amounts, dates of birth, or calculation results in a database.

Because calculations happen locally, you can close the browser or clear the page without creating an account. You are responsible for keeping any information you choose to copy or save elsewhere secure.

SmartCalc Suite may use browser local storage to remember a display preference, such as whether you selected light or dark mode. This preference is stored on your device and is not a personal financial record.

SmartCalc Suite may use essential technologies for basic operation and, if advertising or analytics are enabled, cookies or similar technologies for measurement, security, frequency control, and relevant advertising. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website features.

Log data is generally used to maintain security, diagnose technical problems, understand general traffic, and prevent abuse.
